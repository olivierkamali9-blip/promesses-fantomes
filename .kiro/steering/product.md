# Promesses Fantômes — Product Steering

## One-liner
Public promises evaporate. Ghost Pledges turns each one into a ghost you can follow until proof arrives.

## Audience
French-speaking Africans first (DR Congo focus), plus anyone following sport, politics or celebrity statements. Mobile-first, low-data, works on slow networks.

## Core mechanic
A "pledge" is a verifiable public claim with a deadline ("Club X wins the cup final", "The road will reopen before 31 Dec").
Players stake **Souls** (in-app points, NO monetary value) on KEPT or BROKEN. Winners share the losing pool in proportion to their stake.

## The ghost IS the state (never decoration)
MURMUR (pending approval, faint) -> APPARITION (open) -> HAUNTING (closes in < 24h, restless) -> resolved:
- KEPT: ghost incarnates (solid, warm light)
- BROKEN: ghost sinks into the Cemetery of Pledges
- INCONCLUSIVE: ghost fades, all stakes refunded
- AI_ERROR: if a human overrides an AI resolution, the AI's mistake is carved on a tombstone in the Cemetery of AI Mistakes (our signature).
Ghost opacity = evidence level (L0 faint ... L4 solid).

## Evidence ladder (shown on every card)
L0 rumor | L1 single statement with link | L2 one reliable source | L3 two independent sources | L4 official document or primary data.
RULE: a pledge can only be resolved at L3 or L4. Otherwise it is INCONCLUSIVE and refunded. Never resolve on one source.

## Safety tiers
- GREEN (sport, scheduled events): AI drafts, publishes and may resolve alone at L3+.
- ORANGE (politics, public pledges, celebrity statements): AI drafts and publishes marked UNCONFIRMED; resolution requires human approval.
- RED (security, armed conflict, ongoing court cases, health emergencies, election results): human approval BEFORE publication.
- BLOCKED (reject with a visible reason): ethnicity or religion, calls to violence, predictions about deaths or attacks, accusations of crimes against private individuals, anything built on invented quotes.

## Honesty rules
- No fabricated quotes. Demo seed data is clearly labelled "Fictional demo scenario".
- Humour targets the PLEDGE, never the person.
- Souls cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn. Any exchange-rate panel is a clearly labelled simulation and is out of scope for the MVP.
- Every AI decision shows its sources and confidence. The AI admits mistakes publicly.

## Voice
Warm, dry, slightly theatrical ghost narrator ("The Oracle"). French by default, English toggle for judges.

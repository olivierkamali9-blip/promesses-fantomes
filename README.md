# Promesses Fantômes (Ghost Pledges)

Public promises evaporate. Ghost Pledges turns each one into a ghost you can follow until proof arrives.
Players stake **Souls** (virtual points, no monetary value). An AI crew drafts, checks and resolves pledges under human-approval safety tiers, and publicly buries its own mistakes in a Cemetery of AI Mistakes.

Category: Costume Contest (Kiroween 2026). Built with Kiro: specs, steering, hooks, MCP, property-based tests.

See `.kiro/specs/promesses-fantomes/` for requirements, design and tasks, and `.kiro/steering/` for product, tech and design rules.
